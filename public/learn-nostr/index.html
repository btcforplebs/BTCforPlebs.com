Nostr Tools & Resources

Explore the ecosystem of Nostr apps and tools available on BTCforPlebs:

Relay

  • BTCforPlebs Relay – A reliable WebSocket relay for your Nostr clients. Connect at wss://relay.btcforplebs.com.

Tools

  • Nsec Bunker – A remote signer that keeps your private keys safe while allowing you to use Nostr apps.
  • NcryptDEsec – A tool for encrypting and decrypting Nostr keys using the NIP-49 standard.

Clients

  • Nostrudel – A feature-rich web client for Nostr, offering a comprehensive social media experience.
  • Coracle – A lightweight and highly customizable Nostr web client.
  • Jumble – A unique Nostr client focused on specific user interactions.
  • Fevela – A social client designed for ease of use and community engagement.

Community

  • Flotilla – A community-focused platform built on Nostr for group discussions and collaboration.

Storage

  • Bloom – A decentralized storage solution replacing Google Drive, powered by Blossom servers.
